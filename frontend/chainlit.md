# Innovation Ways · Grounded RAG

Ask questions about your documents and get answers you can **trust** — grounded
in your sources, cited line-by-line, and filtered by what you're allowed to see.

Every answer carries a **trust badge** and a click-to-open **🔎 How grounded is this?**
panel that shows the retrieval route, the guard checks, the exact sources, and a
link to the full trace. Nothing is asserted that the system can't show you.

| | |
|---|---|
| 🟢 **Grounded** | answered from your documents, with citations |
| ⚪ **General knowledge** | clearly marked as *not* from your documents |
| ⚠️ **Fail-closed** | refuses rather than guess when evidence is thin |

**Fully local · open-weight · single-GPU.** No document, query, or answer ever
leaves the machine — retrieval, generation, guards, and grounding checks all run
on-prem.

> Sign in with your demo account to begin. Use the **⚙️ settings** to switch
> between **assist** (general knowledge allowed) and **strict** (documents-only).
