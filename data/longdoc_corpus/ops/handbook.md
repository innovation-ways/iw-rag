# Operations Handbook (Consolidated)

Section 1. This portion of the handbook concerns meeting-room booking etiquette and the shared calendar conventions. Employees are reminded that the relevant forms live in the intranet under the Operations space, and that any exceptions require sign-off from the floor coordinator before the end of the affected week. Historically these processes were paper-based, but they have since moved to the self-service portal, which tracks each request through draft, review, and resolution states. The facilities team reviews the aggregate metrics monthly and publishes a short summary so that teams can plan around peak demand. When in doubt, prefer the asynchronous channel and attach the reference number so the audit trail stays complete and searchable.
